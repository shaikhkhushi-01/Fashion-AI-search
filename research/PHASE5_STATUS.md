### Evidence boundary
Phase 5 packages the research story; it does not create missing empirical evidence.

Do not claim:
- completed human annotation,
- Fleiss' kappa,
- human-validated retrieval metrics,
- verified multimodal performance,
- generalization to real-world users,

until those experiments have actually been executed with the required evidence.

### Recommended final presentation order
Problem → Research Question → Method → Benchmark → Baselines → Evaluation Protocol → Results → Error Analysis → Human Evaluation → Limitations → Contribution → Future Work.
